A note from Santa

    How the Nice‑o‑Meter counts

    Every answer earns stars. Always gets two, Sometimes gets one, Not yet gets none. Eight questions make sixteen stars at most.

    The meter starts at 60% and every star adds 2.5 points, so the result lands between 60% and 100%.

    A note for grown-ups: the meter never drops below 60%, and the result never tells a child they are on the naughty list. It is a game, with a small nudge toward tidy rooms.

    Questions parents ask

    How do I know if I'm on the Nice List?

    Answer the eight questions on this page. The Nice-o-Meter turns the answers into a score between 60% and 100%. At 75% or more you are on the Nice List. Below that, Santa saves you a spot and asks for two good deeds before Christmas.

    Is the Naughty or Nice test free?

    Yes. The test, the result and the certificate are free. There is no sign-up and no email field.

    Can I print a Nice List certificate?

    Yes. After the last question, type a name and press Print or Save as picture. The certificate fits one landscape page, US Letter or A4.

    Can my child talk to Santa?

    Yes, in the Santa Claus Video Call & Text app for iPhone and iPad. Santa video calls and says your child's name, and kids can chat with him or write him a letter. A reply to a letter usually arrives within an hour. The app is free to download and one letter to Santa a day is free. Video calls come with Premium.

    What if my child is not on the Nice List yet?

    The lowest result is 60%, and the page never says a child is on the naughty list. A lower score comes with two small things to do before Christmas, picked from the answers. Take the test again in a week and watch the needle move.

    Does this page collect my child's name or any data?

    No. The page has no cookies, no analytics and no ads. The name you type is used to draw the certificate in your browser. It is not saved and it is not sent to us or anyone else. A shared result link contains the answers only.
