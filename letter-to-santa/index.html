Questions parents ask

How do I write a letter to Santa online?

Press Start the letter and go through five steps: name and age, how the year went, up to three wishes, one good deed and a question for Santa. The page puts the answers into a finished letter. It takes about two minutes.

Is this letter to Santa template free to print?

Yes. The letter, the picture and the blank template are free. There is no sign-up and no email field. The letter prints on one sheet, US Letter or A4.

Where do I mail a paper letter to Santa?

In the United States, the Postal Service runs the USPS Operation Santa program. Its address for the 2026 season is Santa, 123 Elf Road, North Pole, 88888. Put the letter in an envelope, add a First-Class stamp and write your full name and return address in the upper left corner. Letters must be postmarked by Saturday, December 5, 2026. USPS says that sending a letter does not guarantee it will be posted for adoption, adopted, or that a gift will arrive, and that letters may be published on its site without personal details. The letter from this page carries no surname or address, so a grown-up adds those by hand. We checked these details on uspsoperationsanta.com on November 20, 2026.

No. The page draws the letter and stops there. To mail it, print it and post it yourself. To get an answer, use the Santa Claus Video Call & Text app: kids send Santa a letter there and he writes back.

Can Santa answer my child's letter?

In the Santa Claus Video Call & Text app for iPhone and iPad, yes. Kids write Santa a letter and he replies with the help of his elves. He also video calls and says your child's name. The app is free to download and one letter to Santa a day is free. Video calls come with Premium.

What should a child write in a letter to Santa?

Say hello, tell Santa your name and age, share one good thing you did this year, ask for one to three gifts with the favorite first, and finish with a thank you. A question about the reindeer never hurts.
